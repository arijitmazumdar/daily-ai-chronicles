# Loop Engineering

Loop engineering is the design of bounded, observable, recoverable plan–act–observe–evaluate cycles for agentic systems.

## Related newsletters

- [2026-07-28 — Loop Engineering: Designing Convergent Agent Execution](../newsletters/2026/07/2026-07-28-loop-engineering.md)

## Key concerns

- durable state and checkpointing
- convergence and progress scoring
- termination criteria
- repeated-action detection
- evaluator design
- human approval gates
- idempotent tool execution
- budget and risk controls
